β represents the change in the response per standard deviation of distance. Estimates and 95% confidence intervals were obtained from restricted maximum-likelihood fits using Satterthwaite degrees of freedom. LR denotes likelihood-ratio tests of maximum-likelihood fits against the corresponding model without distance; the interaction test compared the interaction and additive models. These robustness analyses are exploratory, and p-values are unadjusted for multiple testing. The model without the unordered pair retained random intercepts for ordered pair, source species and target species. The ordered-pair-only model omitted the species effects. Adjustment for within-dataset Macro-F1 included source and target performance as covariates and excluded pairs involving Hedgehog. Retention was transfer Macro-F1 divided by the target’s within-dataset Macro-F1; Hedgehog was excluded as a target. Alternative distances replaced the core trait distance in the primary model. EltonTraits variable selection and coding are described in Supplementary Text S2. Trait sensitivity analyses changed one coding decision or omitted one core trait, retaining equal weights for the remaining traits. The cattle neck-length variant was added after the results were known. Singular fit indicates a boundary fit of the random-effects structure. Values are rounded.
