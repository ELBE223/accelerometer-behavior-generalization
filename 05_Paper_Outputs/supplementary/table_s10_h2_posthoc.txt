Dataset-level scores were averaged over five training seeds. Difference is the median [first quartile, third quartile] of paired differences, calculated as the first class minus the second. Transfer loss denotes within-dataset minus cross-dataset F1. Tests were two-sided exact Wilcoxon signed-rank tests, with Holm adjustment across the three class comparisons within each classifier and response. Rank-biserial r is the matched-pairs effect size. None of the corresponding Friedman tests remained significant after Holm adjustment across the six classifiers. All post-hoc comparisons are therefore reported descriptively, including those with pairwise Holm-adjusted p-values below 0.05.
