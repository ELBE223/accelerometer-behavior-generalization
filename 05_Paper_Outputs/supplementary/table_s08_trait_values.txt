Body mass was expressed as the natural logarithm of mass in grams. Relative neck length was classified using r = C/(T + L + S), where C, T, L, and S represent cervical, thoracic, lumbar, and sacral vertebral lengths, respectively. Categories were defined as short (r < 0.30), intermediate (0.30 ≤ r < 0.40), long (0.40 ≤ r < 1.00), and very long (r ≥ 1.00). All categorical trait assignments were study-specific. The hedgehog neck-length classification was based on expert judgement, while the raccoon classification relied on indirect anatomical evidence. The five traits were combined using equally weighted Gower distance. Body mass values came from EltonTraits for six taxa, Gerstner and Gerstein (2008) for Horse 1, and study-specific records for Hedgehog and Dog; the Dog value was the unweighted mean of the two study means.
