Each classifier contributed 72 ordered source–target species pairs. β represents the classifier-specific slope of standardized core trait distance in the mixed-effects model containing the distance × classifier interaction. Estimates and confidence intervals were obtained from the restricted maximum-likelihood fit using Satterthwaite degrees of freedom. The overall interaction was not significant (likelihood-ratio χ²(5) = 2.09, p = 0.836). Spearman correlations are descriptive summaries of the species-pair associations. Their confidence intervals were calculated using Fisher’s z transformation, and their p-values were Holm-adjusted across the six classifiers. These correlations do not account for the dyadic dependence represented in the mixed-effects model.
